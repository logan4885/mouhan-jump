Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026. This policy covers the game "Jump 3D" at jump.mouhan.dev, made by Mouhan ("we").

What we store

Playing needs no account. To run the leaderboard we store: a random player ID, a nickname you choose, your best score, number of perfect landings and games played, and which cans you have unlocked or equipped. We do not collect your real name, address or location, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We only store a phone number if you choose to enter your WhatsApp number (see below).

Optional sign-in with Google or Facebook

If you choose to sign in, Google or Facebook tells us a unique user identifier for your account. We store only that identifier (linked to your player ID) so you can get your progress back on another device. We do not store your email address, password, profile picture or friends list. Your display name is shown to you in the game on your own device only.

On your device

The game keeps your progress, settings and a secret device key in your browser's local storage. Clearing browser data removes them; signing in lets you restore your progress.

Optional WhatsApp number (free skin)

If you choose to enter your WhatsApp number in exchange for a free skin, you must be signed in and tick a consent box. We store the number (with your player ID, nickname, language and the date) so we can contact you on WhatsApp about events, new skins and game updates. We only check the number's format; we do not verify it. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP on WhatsApp, or by asking us (via mouhan.dev) to delete your number; deleting your player data also deletes it. Skins you have already received are not taken back.

Who can see it

The leaderboard publicly shows your nickname, score and which can you use. Please don't use your real name as a nickname. We do not sell or share your data. Data is stored with our hosting providers (Render and TiDB Cloud, in the EU) only to run the game.

Deleting your data

To have your player record and linked sign-in permanently deleted from our server, ask us via the contact on mouhan.dev (WhatsApp or Instagram), giving your player ID.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

mouhan.dev

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 12 日。本政策适用于 jump.mouhan.dev 上的小游戏《跳一跳 3D》，由牟涵（“我们”）制作。

我们保存什么

玩游戏不需要账号。为了提供排行榜，我们保存：一个随机生成的玩家 ID、你自己填的昵称、最高分、完美落地次数和游戏局数、已解锁和正在使用的罐子。我们不收集你的真实姓名、住址或位置，也不使用广告或追踪 Cookie。只有你主动填写 WhatsApp 号码时，我们才会保存号码（见下文）。

可选的 Google / Facebook 登录

如果你选择登录，Google 或 Facebook 会告诉我们你账号的唯一标识。我们只保存这个标识（并与你的玩家 ID 关联），用于让你在别的设备上找回进度。我们不会保存你的邮箱、密码、头像或好友列表。你的显示名称只在你自己设备上的游戏里显示。

保存在你的设备上

游戏会把进度、设置和一把设备密钥保存在浏览器本地存储里。清除浏览器数据会删除它们；登录后可以恢复进度。

可选：WhatsApp 号码（领皮肤）

如果你选择填写 WhatsApp 号码来领取免费皮肤，需要先登录账号并勾选同意。我们会保存该号码（连同你的玩家 ID、昵称、语言和领取时间），用于通过 WhatsApp 联系你，告知活动、新皮肤和游戏更新。我们只检查号码格式，不做验证。我们不会出售或分享给任何其他人。你可以随时在 WhatsApp 回复 STOP 退订，或通过 mouhan.dev 联系我们删除号码；删除玩家数据时号码也会一并删除。已经领取的皮肤不会收回。

谁能看到

排行榜会公开显示你的昵称、分数和使用的罐子，请不要用真实姓名当昵称。我们不会出售或分享你的数据。数据仅为运行游戏而存放在托管服务商（Render 和 TiDB Cloud，位于欧洲）。

删除你的数据

如需永久删除服务器上你的玩家记录和已绑定的登录，请通过 mouhan.dev 上的联系方式（WhatsApp 或 Instagram）提供玩家 ID，我们会代为删除。

儿童

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会有意收集其个人信息。
